# 09 — Legal, Compliance & Risk

This is a real business with real rules. Reading this is what separates operators
who build a durable income from those who get their assets shut down. **None of
this is legal advice — consult a licensed attorney/accountant for your
situation.**

---

## 1. Google Business Profile — play it straight
- **Do not create fake/fictitious GBP listings.** "Lead-gen" listings for a
  business that doesn't really operate at a real address violate Google's
  guidelines and get **suspended or banned** — often taking your work with it.
- **Legitimate paths:**
  - Operate the lead-gen entity as a **real business** with a verifiable
    presence and accurate info, **or**
  - **Partner first**, then build/optimize the GBP *with and for* a real local
    contractor (co-managed), **or**
  - Lean on **website SEO + paid ads + LSA**, which don't depend on a GBP you
    can't legitimately verify.
- Keep **NAP consistent** and truthful everywhere. Don't keyword-stuff the
  business name.

## 2. Call recording & consent
- Some states require **two-party (all-party) consent** to record calls (e.g.
  CA, FL, PA, IL, WA, and others). Many are one-party.
- Use a call-tracking provider that handles **consent announcements** ("this call
  may be recorded") and configure per state.
- Build the recording/consent terms into your renter agreement.

## 3. Advertising & lead compliance
- **TCPA / telemarketing:** if you do SMS/cold-call outreach, honor opt-outs,
  respect calling hours, and check Do-Not-Call rules. Keep B2B outreach
  professional and easy to opt out of.
- **Google/Meta ad policies:** advertise truthfully; don't imply you *are* the
  contractor if you're generating leads for one. Misrepresentation gets ad
  accounts banned.
- **LSA / "Google Guaranteed":** requires real license/insurance verification for
  many trades — don't fake it.

## 4. Licensing & "who is the business?"
- Many trades (HVAC, roofing, electrical, restoration, pest control) are
  **licensed**. *You* aren't performing the work — your **renter** must hold the
  proper license/insurance. Make that their contractual responsibility.
- Your websites shouldn't claim licenses/certifications you (or the renter) don't
  hold. Represent the service accurately.

## 5. Contracts & exclusivity
- Put it in writing (see [`templates/rank-and-rent-agreement.md`](../templates/rank-and-rent-agreement.md)):
  ownership of assets stays with **you**; the renter gets an **exclusive license**
  to leads; billing, qualified-lead definition, cancellation, and non-payment
  remedies are explicit.
- **Don't over-promise.** Sell *access to leads*, not guaranteed jobs or revenue.
  Avoid income guarantees.

## 6. Business setup & taxes
- Form an **LLC**, get an EIN, open a business bank account, keep clean books.
- Track income/expenses from day one; set aside for taxes; consult a CPA.
- Use **Stripe** for clean, recordable recurring billing.

## 7. Key business risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Google algorithm/GBP changes | Diversify channels (SEO + ads + LSA + signs); don't rely on one |
| GBP suspension | Stay compliant; co-manage with a real contractor; lean on web/ads |
| Renter stops paying | You own the number/site — reroute leads instantly (kill-switch) |
| Renter mishandles customers | Vet for reviews/reputation; bad service churns your leads |
| Lead quality disputes | Record every call; define "qualified" in the contract |
| Single-client concentration | Build a portfolio; never depend on one renter |
| Ad costs exceed lead value | Watch cost-per-lead daily; shift to organic/GBP over time |
| Seasonality | Mix evergreen (HVAC, pest) with seasonal (storm, roofing) niches |

## 8. Ethics that keep the business durable
This model is win-win **when you actually deliver value**: real leads, to a real
contractor who does real work, for real customers. Generate genuine demand, rent
to operators who serve customers well, represent everything truthfully, and you
build an asset that lasts. Cut corners with fake listings, junk leads, or shady
ads and you build something that gets shut down. **Play the long game.**
