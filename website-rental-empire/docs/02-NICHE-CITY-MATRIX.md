# 02 — The 20 Best Niches × 20 Best Cities

Every ranking below is produced by [`tools/opportunity_score.py`](../tools/opportunity_score.py)
from the datasets in [`data/`](../data/). It scores all **400** niche×city
combinations on a 0–100 scale, weighted toward **speed-to-cash** (rankability +
urgency) without ignoring long-term value (job size) and demand. Re-run it
anytime: `python3 tools/opportunity_score.py`.

> **How to read this:** the *niche* list tells you **what** to sell. The *city*
> list tells you **where**. The **Flagship Portfolio** pairs them into 20
> diversified, geographically-logical assets — your build roadmap.
