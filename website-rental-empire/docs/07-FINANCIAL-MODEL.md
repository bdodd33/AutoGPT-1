# 07 — Financial Model

The numbers behind the business: what it costs, how $6K happens in 30 days, the
unit economics of one asset, and the path to $50K/month.

---

## 1. Startup & monthly costs (lean)

| Item | One-time | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Domain | $12 | — |
| Hosting / site builder | — | $0–25 |
| Call tracking (CallRail/Callsling) | — | $0–45 (free trials exist) |
| Email / outreach tool | — | $0–30 |
| CRM | — | $0 (Google Sheet) |
| Stripe (payments) | — | 2.9% + 30¢ per charge |
| **Ad spend (sprint accelerant)** | — | **$600–1,500** (optional but recommended for speed) |
| **Baseline (no ads)** | **~$12** | **~$30–50** |
| **Sprint mode (with ads)** | **~$12** | **~$650–1,550** |

Koerner's quoted overhead (~$30/month per site) matches the **baseline** row. The
ad spend is a *temporary accelerant* to hit $6K fast — you wind it down as
GBP/SEO take over.

## 2. Five ways to reach $6,000 in 30 days

| Path | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Flat retainers** | 4 clients × $1,500/mo | **$6,000** |
| **B — Pay-per-lead** | 120 qualified leads × $50 | **$6,000** (~4/day) |
| **C — Hybrid (recommended)** | 3 retainers ($4,500) + 30 PPL ($1,500) | **$6,000** |
| **D — Setup + first month** | 3 clients × ($1,000 setup + $1,000) | **$6,000** |
| **E — Asset flip + rent** | Sell 1 ranked site ($3,000) + 2 retainers ($3,000) | **$6,000** |

**Most reliable in month 1 = D blended with C:** the setup fees give you a cash
floor that doesn't depend on lead volume, and the retainers/PPL stack on top.

## 3. Sprint P&L (illustrative, with ads)

```
REVENUE (Month 1)
  3 setup/launch fees @ $1,000 .................. $3,000
  3 first-month retainers @ $1,000 .............. $3,000
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Total collected ............................... $6,000

COSTS (Month 1)
  Domain + hosting + tracking + tools ........... $   90
  Ad spend (3 weeks of ignition) ................ $   900
  Stripe fees (~3%) ............................. $   180
  Signs / flyers ................................ $    50
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Total costs ................................... $ 1,220

NET MONTH 1 ..................................... $ 4,780
Recurring entering Month 2 ..................... $ 3,000+/mo
```

You exit month 1 cash-positive **and** with recurring revenue — then ad spend
drops as organic/GBP leads replace paid ones, widening margin.

## 4. Unit economics of ONE mature asset

```
One ranked water-damage site (post-SEO, ~month 3+):
  Leads generated organically/GBP ....... ~15/month at ~$0 marginal cost
  Rented flat to one contractor ......... $1,500/month
  Maintenance ........................... ~$30/month + ~1 hr/week
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Gross margin .......................... ~98%
  Annual cash flow ...................... ~$18,000
  Resale value (≈20–40× monthly) ........ $30,000–$60,000
```

Each asset is a cash-flowing, sellable digital rental property. That dual return
— monthly rent **plus** a sellable capital asset — is what separates this from
freelancing.

The Flagship Portfolio in [`02-NICHE-CITY-MATRIX.md`](02-NICHE-CITY-MATRIX.md)
models to **≈$41,500/month** fully rented across 20 assets — and that portfolio's
**asset value** (at ~30× monthly) is on the order of **$1.2M+** if you ever
chose to sell.

## 6. Sensitivity / risk-adjusted view

- **Conservative (no ads, slow close):** 2 retainers + setup fees ≈ **$3,500–
  $4,500** month 1, $6K by week 6–7. Still a fast win.
- **Base (this plan):** **$6,000** month 1.
- **Aggressive (ads scale, 5 closes, a flip):** **$8,000–$12,000** month 1.

The lever that moves you between scenarios is **outreach volume + speed of
follow-up**, not luck. Hit 15 contractor touches/day and the base case is highly
achievable.
