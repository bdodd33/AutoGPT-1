# 00 — The Master Plan

*How to build a "rent out websites" business the way Chris Koerner teaches it —
adapted into a concrete, execute-ready system.*

---

## 1. What the business actually is

The model has three honest names. They all describe the same machine:

- **Rank & Rent** — build a website, rank it in Google, rent it out.
- **Local Lead Generation** — generate local service leads, sell them.
- **Website Rental** — Koerner's framing: you're a *digital landlord*.

You are **not** an SEO agency, a web designer, or a marketing consultant. Those
sell *effort by the hour*. You build and own an **asset** — a property that
produces a stream of valuable phone calls — and you **rent the stream** to a
local business that converts those calls into jobs.

> **The mental model:** A billboard on the busiest highway in town is worth money
> because of the eyeballs that pass it. A page ranking #1 for *"emergency water
> removal near me"* is a billboard on the busiest highway in town — except it
> only shows to people who need that exact service *right now*. You own the
> billboard. The restoration company rents the space.

### Why Koerner calls it his #1 business

From his teaching and the operators he's profiled (e.g. the "Website Landlord"
doing ~$104K/month, and his own ~$52K/month across dozens of sites):

- **Tiny overhead.** A site + hosting + tools runs roughly **$30–50/month**.
- **No inventory, no employees, no fulfillment.** The contractor does the work.
- **Recurring revenue.** Rent bills monthly whether or not you touched it.
- **Asset value.** A ranked site can be *sold* for 20–40× monthly rent.
- **Stackable.** Site #2 doesn't cost you site #1's time. The model compounds.
- **A real win for the contractor.** They get jobs with zero marketing effort.

A single deal Koerner has cited: a commercial flat-roofing lead worth a
**$113,750** project, on which his cut was **$5,687**. That is the upside of
high-ticket niches — one lead can be worth more than a month of rent.

## 2. How the money works (the four revenue models)

You will use different models at different stages. Master all four; choose per
deal.

| Model | How you bill | Best for | Typical $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flat monthly rent** | Fixed fee for the site/leads | Established rankings, steady niches (junk removal, pressure washing) | $600–$3,000/mo |
| **Pay-per-lead (PPL)** | Per qualified call/form | New sites, emergency niches, fast start | $25–$150/lead |
| **Pay-per-call** | Per call over X seconds | Phone-driven niches (towing, garage door, auto glass) | $15–$80/call |
| **Revenue share** | % of closed job value | High-ticket (roofing, solar, remodel) where you can track jobs | 5–15% of job |

**Why this matters for a 30-day goal:** flat rent assumes you've *already* got
rankings (slow). **Pay-per-lead and pay-per-call let you start billing the moment
the phone rings** — even from paid ads or yard signs on day 3. That is the lever
the 30-day sprint pulls. See [`07-FINANCIAL-MODEL.md`](07-FINANCIAL-MODEL.md).

## 3. The asset: what you're actually building

For each niche+city you operate, you build a small **"property"** consisting of:

1. **A focused website** — 5–12 pages targeting `{service} {city}` and the
   surrounding towns. Looks like a real local company. Clear phone number, click-
   to-call, a quote form, trust signals, service-area pages.
2. **A Google Business Profile (GBP)** — where possible/legitimate, this is the
   single biggest local ranking + lead lever (the "map pack").
3. **A tracked phone number** — a call-tracking line (CallRail / Callsling) so
   every lead is recorded, attributed, and provable to the renter.
4. **Citations + reviews + content** — the signals that move the site up the
   rankings over weeks 4–12 and make the asset durable.

You build it once. It appreciates. It rents.

## 4. The strategic choices that make or break it

### 4a. Niche selection — the 4 filters
A niche is good when **all four** are true:

1. **High job value** (so the contractor can easily afford rent). A $9,000
   roofing job supports far more rent than a $90 oil change.
2. **Real local search demand** (people Google it before buying).
3. **Beatable competition** (a brand-new site can actually rank — secondary
   cities and "boring" niches are gold).
4. **Urgency / fast close** — for *fast cash*, weight toward emergencies (water
   damage, towing, garage door, HVAC out in July). Urgent searchers call now and
   contractors pay now.

The 20 niches in [`02-NICHE-CITY-MATRIX.md`](02-NICHE-CITY-MATRIX.md) are scored
on exactly these four filters.

### 4b. City selection — the 3 filters
1. **Growth** — Sun Belt / Mountain West metros pulling in new movers = endless
   new service demand (every mover needs movers, cleaners, handymen, lawns).
2. **Demand depth** — enough monthly searches to matter.
3. **Rankability** — *lower competition*. **This is the secret:** competing in
   "Roofing Dallas" is brutal; "Roofing in Greenville SC" or a Dallas *suburb*
   ("Roofing in Frisco TX") is winnable in weeks. **Secondary cities and suburbs
   beat flagship metros for speed-to-rank.**

### 4c. The "boring is beautiful" principle
The best niches are unsexy: junk removal, dumpster rental, foundation repair,
mold, towing, sealcoating. Boring niches have weak incumbent websites, low ad
sophistication, and owners desperate for steady leads. Avoid crowded, agency-
saturated spaces.

## 5. The execution arc (90 days, with the 30-day cash sprint inside it)

```
        ┌─────────────────── DAYS 1–30: THE CASH SPRINT ($6K target) ───────────────────┐
Week 1  │ Pick niche+city · stand up site + GBP + tracking · turn on FAST lead channels   │
Week 2  │ Generate first leads (ads/GBP/yard signs) · line up 5–10 contractor prospects   │
Week 3  │ Close 3–6 contractors on PPL/flat deals · deliver leads · collect first money    │
Week 4  │ Optimize · upsell to monthly retainer · bank $6K · reinvest into asset #2        │
        └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
Days 31–60   SEO compounds: GBP climbs, citations index, reviews flow. Shift PPL → flat rent.
Days 61–90   Asset #1 mostly passive. Launch assets #2–#4. Portfolio approaches $10–15K/mo.
```

The discipline: **Weeks 1–2 you spend to make the phone ring. Weeks 3–4 you sell
the ringing phone.** Don't wait for SEO to sell — sell first, let SEO lower your
costs later.
