# 04 — Lead-Generation Channels (Fast → Durable)

Your asset only pays if the phone rings. This is the full channel stack, ordered
by **how fast it produces leads**. The 30-day sprint leans on the top; the bottom
compounds into passive margin.

| Channel | Time to first lead | Cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Local Services Ads (LSA) | ~3–7 days | Pay-per-lead | Every service niche; "Google Guaranteed" badge |
| Google Search Ads (PPC) | Same day | $30–80/day | Emergency niches, high-intent keywords |
| Google Business Profile (map pack) | 1–4 weeks | Free | The long-term workhorse for local |
| Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads | 1–3 days | $20–50/day | Visual/quote niches (turf, epoxy, remodel, fence) |
| QR-code yard signs & flyers | 1–7 days | ~$0–50 | Near-free local volume (Koerner's tactic) |
| Organic SEO (content + citations) | 4–12 weeks | Time | Durable, passive, margin-fattening |
| Referral / partner stacking | Ongoing | $0 | Compounding word-of-mouth |

---

## 1. Google Local Services Ads (LSA) — the fast pay-per-lead tap
- Appears **above** regular ads with a "Google Guaranteed" badge.
- You're billed **per lead**, not per click — perfect for reselling.
- Requires business verification (license/insurance check for many trades).
- Set your service area to the city + suburbs and your budget to match your sell
  price. If you resell leads at $50 and LSA costs you $25, every lead nets $25 —
  scale that.

## 2. Google Search Ads — the same-day ignition
- Target **emergency, high-intent** keywords: `emergency water removal`,
  `24 hour garage door repair`, `windshield replacement near me`,
  `AC not working {city}`.
- Tight, exact/phrase match + negative keywords (jobs, DIY, salary, parts).
- Call-only or call-extension ads so clicks become calls.
- Start $30–60/day, watch cost-per-lead, kill losers daily, scale winners.
- **This is your guaranteed lead source in week 1** while SEO is asleep.

## 3. Google Business Profile — the durable workhorse
- Drives the majority of local clicks long-term and **costs nothing**.
- Reviews + posts + photos + accurate categories climb the map pack over weeks.
- See the GBP section in [`03-SITE-BUILD-PLAYBOOK.md`](03-SITE-BUILD-PLAYBOOK.md).

## 4. Meta Ads — for visual / considered-purchase niches
- Best for turf, epoxy floors, remodels, fences, concrete — things people *see*
  and want.
- Lead-form ads (instant forms) get cheap leads fast; retarget site visitors.
- Pair a before/after image with a "Free Quote in {City}" CTA.

## 5. QR-code yard signs & flyers — the near-free Koerner tactic
Chris Koerner specifically teaches **QR-code yard signs** to capture free leads
from local foot/▪car traffic. Cheap, fast, local, and underused.
- Print signs/flyers with a bold offer + **QR code → your quote page**.
- Place at busy intersections (where legal/permitted), hardware-store boards,
  laundromats, community boards, gyms, gas stations; door-hangers in target
  neighborhoods.
- For storm/emergency niches, blanket the affected area after weather events.
- Track with a dedicated URL/UTM so you know signs are working.
- **Cost: a few dollars per sign. Leads: free after that.** Great margin booster
  in a tight month.

## 6. Organic SEO — the compounding asset
- Slow to start, but it's what makes the business **passive and high-margin**.
- Service-area pages + steady reviews + citations + a little local link-building
  lift you over weeks 4–12, after which leads cost ~$0 and the rent is pure
  margin. (Details in [`03-SITE-BUILD-PLAYBOOK.md`](03-SITE-BUILD-PLAYBOOK.md).)

## 7. Referral / partner stacking — free compounding
- Adjacent contractors trade leads (a plumber refers water-damage jobs; a
  realtor refers junk removal/cleaning).
- Ask every happy renter for a referral to a contractor in a *different* niche
  you can build next.

---

## The sprint channel mix (first 30 days)

```
Week 1: Search Ads + LSA + QR yard signs   ← ignite leads in days
Week 2: + GBP starts surfacing, tune ad CPL ← prove volume, lower cost
Week 3: keep all on while you CLOSE clients  ← leads = sales proof
Week 4: SEO/GBP rising → begin shifting cost off paid ads
```

**Cost-per-lead vs. sell-price is the whole game.** If paid channels deliver
leads at $20–30 and you rent/sell them at $50–150 (or roll them into a
$1,500/mo flat retainer), you're profitable from week one and *increasingly*
profitable as free organic/GBP leads replace paid ones.
