# Cold SMS & Voicemail Scripts

Phone/SMS outconverts email for local contractors. Keep it human and short.
**Honor opt-outs, respect calling hours, and check TCPA/DNC rules** — see
`docs/09-LEGAL-AND-RISK.md`. Fill in `{Service}`, `{City}`, `{FirstName}`,
`{YourName}`, `{Number}`.

---

## SMS — first touch
> Hi {FirstName}, it's {YourName}. I run a {Service} site in {City} that gets
> homeowner calls. Looking for one local company to send leads to — first few
> free. Taking new {Service} jobs right now? Reply STOP to opt out.

## SMS — live lead (the closer)
> {FirstName} — got a {Service} lead in {City} right now, homeowner wants a quote
> today. Want it? I'll text you their info. – {YourName}

## SMS — follow-up
> Hey {FirstName}, still want first crack at my {City} {Service} leads? Lining up
> one company this week. 15-min call? – {YourName}

## SMS — exclusivity nudge
> {FirstName}, finalizing the one {Service} company I send {City} leads to. Want
> it to be you before I call the next shop? – {YourName}

---

## Cold call opener (15 seconds)
> "Hey {FirstName}, this is {YourName} — I'll be quick. I run a {Service} website
> in {City} that gets calls from homeowners needing work done. I'm looking for
> one local company to send those leads to, and I'll send the first couple free
> so you can see they're real. Are you guys taking new {Service} jobs right now?"

**If yes →** "Perfect. I'll route the next one to you today. If you like them, we
do a simple month-to-month deal — one company per city, exclusive. Sound fair?"

**If "how much?" →** "Depends on volume, but the rule I use is you make about 10x
what you pay. I'll show you real recorded calls first — judge the quality, then
we talk numbers. Cool?"

---

## Voicemail (20 seconds)
> "Hi {FirstName}, {YourName} here. I generate {Service} leads in {City} and I'm
> picking one local company to send them to — exclusively. I'll send the first
> few free so you can see they're real. Call or text me back at {Number}. Thanks!"

---

## Cadence
Day 0: call + voicemail + SMS · Day 2: SMS · Day 4: call + voicemail ·
Day 6: SMS (exclusivity) · Day 9: final SMS. **The live-lead text closes more
deals than anything else — use it the moment you have a real lead in hand.**
