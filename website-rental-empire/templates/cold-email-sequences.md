# Cold Email Sequences — Booking the Renter

Fill in `{Service}`, `{City}`, `{FirstName}`, `{YourName}`, `{Number}`,
`{SiteURL}`. Keep it short, proof-led, one CTA. Send from a real, professional
inbox. Always include an easy opt-out (compliance — see `docs/09-LEGAL-AND-RISK.md`).

---

## Sequence A — "Free leads" offer (default, highest converting)

### Email 1 — Day 0 (subject: `{Service} leads in {City}`)
> Hi {FirstName},
>
> I run a {Service} website that ranks in {City} and gets calls from homeowners
> who need help now. I'm looking for **one** local {Service} company to send
> these leads to — exclusively.
>
> I'll send you the **first few free** so you can see the quality. If they turn
> into jobs, we set up a simple month-to-month arrangement. No long contract.
>
> Want me to route the next one to you? Reply "yes" or call/text me at {Number}.
>
> — {YourName}
> *Don't want these emails? Reply "stop" and I won't follow up.*

### Email 2 — Day 3 (subject: `re: {Service} leads in {City}`)
> Hi {FirstName},
>
> Quick follow-up — I had a {Service} lead come in from {neighborhood/zip}
> yesterday. I want to make sure they're going to a shop that'll take care of
> them.
>
> Are you taking new {Service} jobs in {City} right now? If so I'll send the next
> one your way, no charge, so you can judge for yourself.
>
> — {YourName}

### Email 3 — Day 6 (subject: `picking one {Service} company in {City}`)
> Hi {FirstName},
>
> I'm finalizing the **one** {Service} company I'll send my {City} leads to. I'd
> rather it be you, but I'm reaching out to a couple of others this week.
>
> 15-minute call to lock it in? Here's my calendar: {CalendlyLink}. Or just reply
> with a good time.
>
> — {YourName}

## Tips
- **Personalize line 1** (mention their reviews, truck, a recent job) — lifts
  replies a lot.
- **One ask per email.** Reply "yes," or book the call. Never two CTAs.
- **Follow up 4–6 times** across email + SMS + call. Most deals close on touch
  3–5.
- **Lead with proof** (recorded calls) the moment you have it — it out-converts
  every clever subject line.
