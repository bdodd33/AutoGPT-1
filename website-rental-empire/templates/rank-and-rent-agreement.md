**LEAD GENERATION & EXCLUSIVE LICENSE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on **{Date}** between:

- **Provider:** {YourLegalName / LLC}, ("Provider") — owner of the website,
  phone number(s), and marketing assets described below; and
- **Client:** {ClientLegalName / Business}, ("Client") — a licensed and insured
  provider of {Service} services in {City/Area}.

### 1. The Asset
Provider owns and operates the website **{SiteURL}**, the tracking phone
number(s) **{Number}**, associated profiles/listings, and all related rankings,
content, and marketing ("the Asset"). **Provider retains sole ownership** of the
Asset, including the domain, phone numbers, listings, content, and any goodwill,
at all times during and after this Agreement.

### 2. What Client Receives
Provider grants Client an **exclusive license** to receive the customer leads
(calls and form submissions) generated by the Asset for **{Service}** in
**{City/Area}** ("Leads") for the Term. Exclusive = Provider will not sell these
specific Leads to another {Service} company in {City/Area} while this Agreement
is active and Client is current on payment.

### 3. Fees & Billing
- **One-time setup/launch fee:** **${SetupFee}**, due on signing.
- **Recurring fee (choose one):**
  - ☐ **Flat monthly rent:** **${MonthlyRent}/month**, billed in advance on the
    {1st} of each month; **or**
  - ☐ **Pay-per-lead:** **${PerLead}** per Qualified Lead, billed
    {weekly/monthly} in arrears.
- Payment via {Stripe/Invoice}. Auto-renews monthly unless cancelled per §6.

### 4. Qualified Lead (for pay-per-lead deals)
A "Qualified Lead" is a call lasting longer than **{30/60} seconds** from a
prospective customer in the service area, or a form submission with valid contact
details requesting **{Service}**. **Not** qualified (no charge): spam, robocalls,
wrong numbers, solicitations, out-of-area, duplicates within {14} days, or calls
under the minimum duration. Provider records calls for verification; disputes
raised within **{5}** days will be reviewed in good faith.

### 5. Client Responsibilities
Client represents that it holds all required **licenses, permits, and insurance**
to perform {Service}, will respond to Leads **promptly and professionally**, will
serve customers lawfully and to industry standard, and is solely responsible for
the work performed and any customer relationship arising from a Lead.

### 6. Term, Renewal & Cancellation
Month-to-month, beginning {StartDate}. Either party may cancel with **{30} days'**
written notice. On termination, the exclusive license ends and Provider may route
the Leads elsewhere. The Asset and all its components remain Provider's property.

### 7. Non-Payment
If payment is **{5}** days past due, Provider may **suspend or reroute** the Leads
without liability until the account is brought current. Continued non-payment
terminates the license.

### 8. No Guarantee
Provider supplies **access to Leads generated by the Asset**, not a guarantee of
lead volume, lead conversion, jobs, or revenue. Marketing performance varies.
Provider is not responsible for Client's close rate or business results.

### 9. Call Recording & Compliance
Client consents to Provider's recording of calls to the tracking number for
quality and verification, subject to applicable law. Each party will comply with
all applicable advertising, telemarketing, privacy, and consumer-protection laws.

### 10. Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential damages. Provider's total liability is limited to fees paid by
Client in the **{prior 1 month}**.

### 11. Independent Contractors
The parties are independent contractors. Nothing creates a partnership, agency,
employment, or joint venture. Client is not authorized to act on Provider's
behalf, and vice versa.

### 12. General
This Agreement is the entire agreement, governed by the laws of **{State}**, and
may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
