# MWFaith — 20 Christian T-Shirt Designs

Twenty launch designs engineered from the **top-selling Christian apparel design categories for 2026**: minimalist "quiet faith," scripture typography, vintage/acid-wash streetwear, visual metaphor, Greek/Hebrew language, and women's floral/country-faith. Each maps to an avatar from the Brand Strategy doc.

> Designs are grouped into the three storefront collections used by the theme: **Minimalist Faith**, **Scripture & Verse**, and **Streetwear Drops** (plus a Women's capsule woven across).
> A ready-to-import Shopify CSV of all 20 lives at `docs/mwfaith-products.csv`.

Legend — **Blank:** B3001 = Bella+Canvas 3001 · CC = Comfort Colors garment-dyed · HVY = heavyweight oversized · HOOD = 350–400 GSM hoodie.

## Launch sequencing recommendation

1. **Week 1 (Signature drop):** #1 Cross-Minimal, #6 Faith Over Fear, #12 Jesus Is King, #13 Lion & the Lamb, #19 Wildflowers — covers all four avatars and three collections.
2. **Week 2–3:** Fill out Minimalist Faith + Scripture & Verse.
3. **Monthly limited drops:** Streetwear (#16 Acid-Wash, language tees #14/#15) to drive TikTok urgency and rewards engagement.

Each product should ship with: 2–3 lifestyle photos (black + vintage white lead), a flat mockup, fabric/care accordion content (pre-filled in the theme), and the verse/context in the description for SEO.
