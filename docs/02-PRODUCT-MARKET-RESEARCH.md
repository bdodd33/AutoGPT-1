# MWFaith — T-Shirt Market Research & Blank Selection

This document covers the **best-performing T-shirt blanks** for print-on-demand in 2026, recommended **colors**, **fits**, **quality benchmarks**, and the **MWFaith blank strategy** that the 20-design catalog is built on.

---

## 1. Best-selling POD T-shirt blanks (2026)

| Blank | Best for | Weight / feel | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bella+Canvas 3001** | Premium all-rounder, lifestyle/luxury launches | 4.2 oz Airlume combed & ring-spun cotton, retail fit | Smooth print surface, 30+ colors, XS–4XL. **MWFaith core blank.** |

---

## 2. Best-selling colors (2026)

- **Evergreen bestsellers:** Black, White, Navy, Red, Gray. **Black is the #1 seller** — pairs with any design and reads premium.
- **2026 trend colors:** Pistachio (light olive/sage-adjacent), Ice Blue, washed/garment-dyed earth tones (sand, clay, moss).
- **MWFaith default color ladder per design:** Black → Vintage White/Ivory → Sand/Bone → Moss/Olive → Navy. (Lead with black + ivory in photography.)

---

## 3. Fits that sell

1. **Unisex relaxed/retail** (Bella 3001) — safe default, widest appeal.
2. **Oversized / dropped-shoulder** — streetwear avatar, premium price.
3. **Garment-dyed boxy** (Comfort Colors) — boutique/country-faith avatar.
4. **Women's relaxed/cropped** — Mia & Hannah avatars.

---

## 4. Design trends driving sales (2026)

- **Minimalism / "Quiet Faith":** clean type, small cross/dove, monochrome/tonal prints on premium fabric. Highest perceived value.
- **Vintage & acid/garment wash:** strong on Amazon/Shein; key for streetwear.
- **Scripture typography:** single verse, single line, or reference-only (e.g., *Phil 4:13*).
- **Language depth:** Hebrew/Greek words (e.g., *Ἰχθύς / Ichthys*, *Hesed*), Latin, to add intrigue and "conversation starter" value.
- **Visual parables / metaphor:** Lion & the Lamb, vine & branches, anchor (hope), light/dark.
- **Acronyms & wordplay:** wholesome, relatable, shareable (great for TikTok).
- **Country-western + faith** and **coquette/floral + faith** for women's segments.

## 6. MWFaith blank strategy (recommendation)

- **Core catalog → Bella+Canvas 3001** for consistency, color range, and print quality.
- **Garment-dyed capsule → Comfort Colors 1717** for the boutique/country-faith looks.
- **Streetwear drops → heavyweight oversized** tees + 350–400 GSM hoodies.
- **Bulk/event → Gildan 5000** only when price is the deciding factor.
- Lead every product page with **black and vintage white** photography; offer the 5-color ladder above.
